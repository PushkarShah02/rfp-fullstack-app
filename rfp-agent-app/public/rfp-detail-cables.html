1. Introduction and Purpose

National Power Grid (NPG) invites e-tenders from reputed ISO certified firms for the "Preparation of Detailed Project Report (DPR) and subsequent execution of Underground Cabling Works (LT, 11kV & 33kV) of the existing distribution infrastructure network in different locations under the National Grid Western Expansion Project."

This initiative is part of NPG's plan to create a disaster-resilient power distribution infrastructure for better reliability, capacity, safety, and improved stability of power. This RFB is for the supply, installation, testing, and commissioning of all materials as specified in the Bill of Quantities (BOQ).

2. Scope of Work

The scope of work for the successful bidder shall include, but not be limited to, the following:

  • A detailed field survey (GPS survey) to establish the feasibility and finalize the routes for the underground cables.
  • Preparation of a Detailed Project Report (DPR) including layout drawings, cable sizing calculations, and protection coordination.
  • Supply of all materials as specified in the BOQ, including cables, Ring Main Units (RMUs), feeder pillars, and jointing kits.
  • All civil works, including trenching, ducting, and backfilling as per IS standards.
  • Installation, testing, and commissioning of the entire underground network.
  • Handing over and providing "as-built" drawings and documentation.

3. Minimum Eligibility Criteria

a) Technical Requirements

  • The firm must have 5+ years of experience in providing Power Distribution Sector consultancy or turnkey execution services.
  • The firm must have undertaken at least three (3) similar assignments for underground cable distribution networks in the last five (5) financial years.
  • The firm must be ISO 9001 certified.

b) Financial Requirements

  • Average annual turnover for the last three consecutive financial years shall be as per NIT.
  • Net worth for the last financial year must be positive.
